Discuss Your Project

Tell us what you have, what is not working, and what "done" would look like for you. The first step is always an assessment, not a quote.

Useful to include

  • What the software or process is supposed to do.
  • What exists today (repository, prototype, spreadsheet, nothing yet).
  • What fails, and since when.
  • Constraints: deadline, budget range, hosting, data sensitivity.
  • Which offer seems closest: S01 Automation, S02 MVP or S03 AI Code Rescue.

Contact channel

Send a brief description of your project to lughlammas@gmail.com. A person will review your message before replying.

Please do not include passwords or customer data in your first message. There is no guaranteed response time.

We use the information you email only to assess your inquiry and reply. Your email service handles delivery; do not send confidential material before we agree on a secure channel.
